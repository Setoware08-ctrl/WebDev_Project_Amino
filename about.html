History

Sofia Marteniz is the who came up with the idea of Anime Haven. A lifelong anime enjoyer, she wanted to create a space where anime fans could share their love for the medium.As a little girl, the first anime she ever watched as called Smile Precure , a japanese television made by Toei Animation.


The show inspired her, teaching her lessons about how to say postive in dark times and how to be a good, supportive friend.
